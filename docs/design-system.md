# Relay · Design system

## Direction

An offline-first Windows control surface with a calm, human technical tone. The visual system uses generous spacing, neutral surfaces, precise hierarchy, and rounded geometry without copying OpenAI marks, logos, or product identity.

## Identity

- Primary asset: `gui/assets/relay-radar.png`
- Icon: white minimalist radar on a full black field
- Signature motion: audio waveform only while audio is playing
- Product name: Relay

## Color

- Light canvas: `#f4f4f1`
- Light surface: `#ffffff`
- Light ink: `#111110`
- Dark canvas: `#10100f`
- Dark surface: `#181817`
- Dark ink: `#f5f5f1`
- Success is the only persistent status accent

## Interface

- Four focused workspaces: Overview, Media, Overlay, History
- English and French switch, persisted locally
- Light and dark theme switch, persisted locally
- Images and GIFs use the configured timer
- Video and audio play to completion
- Skip is always available via button and global `Ctrl+Alt+S`
- Media volume starts at 50 percent and is stored in app configuration

## Constraints

- No OpenAI logo, Blossom, wordmark, or implied affiliation
- No decorative gradients, fake metrics, or filler cards
- Minimum control height: 39 px; primary transport control: 44 px
- Transparent overlay while idle
